# Unit 7 Teacher Guide: vocabulary for Lessons 1-3

Source: Core Knowledge Language Arts, Grade 3, Unit 7 Teacher Guide (Lessons 1-3). CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, Core Knowledge Foundation.

### Lesson 1 vocabulary: "The Sun, Earth, and Our Solar System"

planet: a round object in space that orbits a star (planets). Reader page 10.

orbit: the curved path something in space takes around another object in space; planets move in an orbit around the sun (orbiting). Reader page 10.

solar system: the sun, other bodies like asteroids and meteors, and the planets that orbit the sun. Reader page 10.

rotate: to turn about an axis or a center (rotating, rotates, rotation). Reader page 12.

axis: an imaginary straight line through the middle of an object, around which that object spins. Reader page 12.

tilted: slanted or tipped to one side. Reader page 14.

### Lesson 2 vocabulary: "The Moon"

eclipse: the blocking of the light from the sun by another heavenly body (eclipses). Reader page 20.

### Lesson 3 vocabulary: "The Planets Closest to the Sun: Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars"

naked eye: your eye. Reader page 24.

probe: a tool used to explore something, such as outer space (probes). Reader page 30.
